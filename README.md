# Ensemble reconstruction of the Greenland Ice Sheet evolution through the last deglaciation

Processing the ensemble of Greenland ice sheet simulations for the last deglaciation from Tabone et al. (2024) and comparing them against different proxies.

The proxies and measurements against which the ensemble is compared are processed in the [FesmData](https://github.com/fesmc/FesmData/) repository. These include:

| Observable | Original data for comparison | Source |
|-----------|------------------------------|--------|
| H (present-day ice thickness) | BedMachine v3 | [Morlighem et al. (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1038/ngeo2167) |
| f_ice (present-day ice mask/cover) | BedMachine v3 | [Morlighem et al. (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1038/ngeo2167) |
| z_bed (present-day bedrock elevation) | BedMachine v3 | [Morlighem et al. (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1038/ngeo2167) |
| u (present-day surface velocities) | GIMP | [Joughin et al. (2018)](https://doi.org/10.5067/OC7B04ZM9G6Q) |
| iso (extent isochrones, i.e., margin retreat) | PaleoGrIS v1.0 | [Leger et al. (2024)](https://cp.copernicus.org/articles/20/701/2024/) |
| GRIP (elevation change at GRIP) | Reconstruction data | [Vinther et al. (2009)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08355) |
| NGRIP (elevation change at NGRIP) | Reconstruction data | [Vinther et al. (2009)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08355) |
| DYE3 (elevation change at DYE-3) | Reconstruction data | [Vinther et al. (2009)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08355) |
| LGM (LGM maximum ice extension) | Mask between continental-shelf break and first isochrone | — |
